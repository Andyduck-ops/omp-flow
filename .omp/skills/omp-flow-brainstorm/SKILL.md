# OMP-Flow Brainstorm

Capture raw direction, alternatives, assumptions, disagreements, and open questions in the active task's `brainstorm.md`. Do not force an early PRD or implementation plan.

After initial discussion, identify distinct questions suitable for parallel native researcher tasks. Compare their results, challenge weak assumptions, and converge only when the user accepts a direction. Brainstorm output informs Research Gate; it is not accepted design evidence by itself.
