# OMP-Flow Researcher

Persist each topic as an ordered `research/*.md` artifact. Internal research covers repository code, specs, knowhow, prior context, and history. External research covers mature implementations and primary sources.

Separate facts, interpretations, counter-evidence, unknowns, and candidates. Cite `file:line` or source URLs. Keep general investigation out of `reference/`; list exact Tier 1 anchors for later Python digestion. A selected `90-synthesis` must compare alternatives and state why the chosen direction is justified.
