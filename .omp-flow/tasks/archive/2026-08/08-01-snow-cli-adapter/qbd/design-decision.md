# QbD 1 human calibration

On 2026-08-01, the human accepted the independent [QbD 1 audit](design-audit-2.md) as `PASS` and
approved the current [PRD](../prd.md) and [Design](../design.md) to proceed to decomposition.

The recorded calibration is that the identified Snow native-agent identity limits, Cursor session
lifecycle verification, and project Hook behavior are normal implementation concerns and are not
material design blockers. The audit's moderate residual risk is accepted for this scope.

The advisory items remain useful implementation and verification guidance: keep unsupported exact
native dispatch visibly unavailable, validate Cursor lifecycle boundaries on a released runtime,
use the documented Cursor identity field, bind combined-install host selection to runtime evidence,
and describe Snow project Hook precedence truthfully. They do not require a design revision or a
fresh QbD 1 audit before decomposition.
