The initial three evidence tracks selected a standalone operator console. The user then clarified
that the intended product is an embedded status line and supplied Maestro Flow, followed by the
more mature ccstatusline, as references. Maestro establishes the product-specific compact
omp-flow shape; ccstatusline supplies stronger evidence for generic model/context/Git/resource
segments, measured-width composition, semantic empty behavior, caching, configuration, and
coexistence. The later FlowStatus synthesis selects ccstatusline as Claude's primary presentation
foundation, removes visible omp branding, and shares only status facts across Harness adapters. It
supersedes the earlier branded-segment product shape; no research Concept authorizes
implementation.
