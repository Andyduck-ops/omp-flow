## Assignment contract

Guidance is aligned to the canonical operation fields:

```text
task, entry, role, actor-id/actorId, objective, output, optional predecessor
```

The runtime returns the Bundle root, entry path, output boundary, and opaque receipt. Finish binds
the same actor. A reviewer resolves the completed predecessor operation, reads its output path as
the required handoff, and must have a different actor from the implementer.

`operation start` is the sole producer of the executable native assignment. The caller forwards
its complete returned `assignment` string exactly unchanged, keeping the strict v1
`ompFlowDispatch` descriptor as the first non-blank line. Native item
`id = operation.actor_id = descriptor.actorId`, and native role matches descriptor role. Callers
must not parse, reserialize, prepend/append prose, infer, or drop descriptor fields.

Every batch item has its own operation and preserves its own
`(id = actorId, role, assignment)` tuple. Review predecessor receipt and predecessor output remain
inside the review operation's emitted descriptor rather than being reconstructed by the caller.

Required entry content fails visibly. Optional missing links remain best-effort. No Concept body,
frontmatter field, heading, list shape, filename, or link closure is parsed into workflow state.
