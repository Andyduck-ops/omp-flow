## Contract review

- Two different operations cannot claim the same receipt: only the exclusive creator succeeds,
  and a later claimant receives `WorkflowError`.
- A retry by the same operation is accepted only when the existing claim is readable and carries
  that exact `operation_id`.
- A zero-length, partial, or otherwise unreadable claim is not deleted or reclaimed. Parsing fails
  visibly, preserving fail-closed behavior after an interrupted exclusive create.
- The claim is retained if writing or syncing fails, avoiding automatic reuse when the external
  action may already have occurred.
- Receipt claims and operation records remain mechanical runtime data under
  `.omp-flow/.runtime`; they contain no Markdown semantics, topology, verdict prose, or lifecycle
  interpretation.
- Operation entry paths are confined to the selected Bundle; actor and role identity are required;
  operation IDs are opaque UUID-derived values.
- The canonical task scaffold publishes only `index.md`, `task.md`, and `brainstorm.md`, declares
  OKF v0.2, and does not seed the retired ledgers or placeholder directories.

  `.omp-flow/scripts` has no diff.
