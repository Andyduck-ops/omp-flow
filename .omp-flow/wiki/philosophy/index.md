# Working philosophy

- [Evidence-led exploration](evidence-led-exploration.md) — accumulate connected evidence before
  synthesis and keep rigor proportional to the decision.
- [Semantic knowledge, mechanical control](semantic-knowledge-mechanical-control.md) — let
  documents and links carry meaning, let agents interpret it, and keep code focused on
  irreducible mechanical guarantees.
- [Observable methodology without lifecycle state](observable-flow-without-lifecycle-state.md) —
  show the selected root task, reversible Flow focus, meaningful iteration counts, and honest
  progress without inventing a lifecycle database.
