# Human–Agent Learn

Learn is the repository space for deliberate, human-present Human–Agent co-learning: provisional
digestion, explanation, challenge, correction, and reflection that may help both participants revise
their understanding. Production work normally pauses while a Learn conversation is underway and
resumes only when the human returns the conversation to production.

## Stable affordances

- [Daily](daily/index.md) preserves a useful checkpoint from one dated co-learning encounter.
- [Threads](threads/index.md) carries a shared model, live disagreement, and useful links across
  encounters when continuity is actually needed.
- [Retrospectives](retrospectives/index.md) reflects on a substantive change, problem, or lesson in
  technical understanding, human digestion, Agent explanation or assumptions, or collaboration.

These indexes are navigation aids, not exhaustive manifests. The directories are distinct stable
capabilities, not a required companion set: a Daily may stand alone, and a Thread or Retrospective
is created only when the learning warrants it.

## Dated bundles

Place a bundle immediately below the relevant stable directory and name it
`YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>/`, using its establishment date and a readable topic. Every bundle has an
authored `index.md` that gives a returning human and Agent the useful current understanding or open
question and links to related Concepts only when those links aid resumption.

A bundle may also contain concrete, descriptive Markdown Concepts warranted by the discussion,
such as a worked example, counterargument, shared model, explanation to revisit, open question, or
reflection. There is no required companion bundle, transcript, fixed body schema, lifecycle,
machine-validated identifier, or exhaustive bundle index.

## Authority boundary

Learn is provisional authored context. It does not advance, pause-state, approve, or otherwise
alter an omp-flow Task; Task commitments remain in the applicable Task Bundle. It is not Wiki
authority and is never promoted automatically; established knowledge reaches the Wiki only through
ordinary evidence-based Wiki maintenance. Ordinary links to Task or Wiki Concepts provide context,
not authority transfer.
