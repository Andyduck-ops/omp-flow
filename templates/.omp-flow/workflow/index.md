# Workflow Library

Every Workflow Task starts with `task create`. Read the Workflow that matches the work's main
question, consequence, and verifier; the Task Bundle records the purpose, boundaries, Concepts,
assignment, evidence, and result.

## Workflows

- [Full Delivery](full-delivery.md) — complete product work from framing through design, human
  decisions, bounded implementation, independent review, integration, and archive.
- [Lite](lite.md) — a local reversible change with a known verifier and small decision surface.
- [Research](research.md) — evidence, comparison, explanation, or synthesis before a product change.
- [Experiment](experiment.md) — fixed inputs, controlled comparison, repeated trials, and measured
  results, including failures and negative findings.
- [Workflow Maintenance](workflow-maintenance.md) — evidence-led candidates for improving this
  authored library.

The links are authored navigation. Workflow documents guide reasoning and evidence; Runtime and
Harnesses continue to own session, assignment, actor identity, execution, and atomic side effects.
